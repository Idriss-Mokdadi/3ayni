  • Eye-gaze devices cost thousands of dinars
  • Silent distress goes unnoticed

How it works

From a glance to the nurse's pocket

  1. Camera

    Films the face, on this laptop only

  2. Face model

    Tracks the eyes and 478 face points

  3. Six tiles

    The gaze selects one large tile

  4. Pulse

    Skin colour changes reveal the heartbeat

  5. Rules

    Combine the words and the heart rate

  6. Phone

    Salma gets the message and the alert

Nothing leaves the laptop but words and numbers.
